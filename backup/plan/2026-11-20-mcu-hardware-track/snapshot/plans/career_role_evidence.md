## Recommended Emphasis

Primary: Senior/Lead/Staff MCU firmware, RTOS/peripheral drivers and platform/SDK
development. Also consider MCU applications/customer engineering where the work
and compensation fit. The learner is open to either; do not infer a title/pay
preference from the earlier plan. Principal is selective, based on actual scope.

Reported professional evidence: AURIX driver cyclic functions, motor-control/BMS
IC communication, AUTOSAR module integration, architecture/interface reviews,
integration tests, requirements traceability and mentoring. FMEA/HAZOP work was
with safety engineers; do not assert independent safety-case ownership.
